Who We Are

About KNG Tax Advisory

KNG Tax Advisory was founded by Kristine Nguyen, CPA, to provide sophisticated tax planning and advisory services tailored to real estate investors, business owners, and entrepreneurs. Our firm combines the technical expertise of a large national accounting firm with the responsiveness and personal attention of a trusted advisor.

With more than 14 years of public accounting experience, Kristine has advised clients ranging from individual investors to large real estate funds and operating partnerships. Her background includes leadership roles at Baker Tilly (formerly Moss Adams) and Deloitte, where she specialized in complex real estate taxation, including partnerships, private equity and real estate funds, syndications, cost segregation strategies, and Real Estate Professional Status (REPS).

At KNG Tax Advisory, you'll work directly with Kristine—not a revolving team of staff. This direct relationship ensures consistent communication, a deep understanding of your business, and proactive tax strategies designed to help you build and preserve long-term wealth.

KNG Tax Advisory is a licensed California Professional Accountancy Corporation based in the San Francisco Bay Area. Kristine is a licensed Certified Public Accountant (CPA) in California and a proud member of the California Society of Certified Public Accountants (CalCPA), committed to maintaining the highest standards of professionalism, ethics, and continuing education while serving clients throughout the United States.
